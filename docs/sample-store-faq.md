# Acme Outdoor Gear — Customer FAQ

> Sample document for the DocuBot demo. Acme Outdoor Gear is a fictional store.

## Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. Orders placed before 2 PM (UTC) on business days are dispatched the same day.

- **United States:** 3–5 business days, free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95
- **Europe and UK:** 5–8 business days, flat rate $12.95
- **Rest of the world:** 7–14 business days, rates calculated at checkout

Every order includes a tracking number sent by email once the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Returns and refunds

You can return unused items in their original packaging within **30 days** of delivery. Start a return from the "My Orders" page and print the prepaid label (US only; international customers pay return shipping).

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item. Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## Warranty

All tents, backpacks and sleeping bags come with a **2-year warranty** against manufacturing defects. Damage from normal wear, accidents or improper use is not covered. To file a claim, email photos of the defect and your order number to support@acme-outdoor.example.

## Product care

- **Tents:** dry completely before storage to prevent mildew. Do not machine wash.
- **Down sleeping bags:** wash with down-specific detergent on a gentle cycle, tumble dry on low with dryer balls.
- **Waterproof jackets:** re-apply DWR coating once a season to keep water beading.

## Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Prices are shown in USD; your bank may apply a currency conversion fee.

## Contact

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM (UTC), via live chat or support@acme-outdoor.example. We reply to emails within one business day.
